# Personas and Journeys

| Reader | Priority and journey | Observable outcome |
|---|---|---|
| PhD supervisor / researcher | Primary: direct localized link → research/topic → project/publication evidence → Research Profile/contact | Understand research question, personal contribution and relevant evidence |
| Employer / collaborator | Secondary: home/projects → detailed case study → architecture/code/demo → CV/contact | Understand execution, decisions and actual role |
| Reader / student | Search/shared URL → article/lesson/resource → references and next item | Read complete content and reach its sources without an account |
| Owner / administrator | Sign in → edit/translate/upload → preview → publish → verify deployed record → revise/remove | Control content and see separate save/publish/deploy states |

Language gateway is only `/`; a direct fa/en link never detours through it. Both audience entry links belong to one identity, not separate portfolios. V1 has no comments, reader accounts, enrollment or active newsletter.
