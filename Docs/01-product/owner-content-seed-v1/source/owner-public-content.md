# 2. Short bio

## English

Taha Mohammadi is a software engineer and researcher whose work sits between engineering, human-computer interaction, and applied AI. Since 2020, he has worked on production software and data systems while developing a research direction around trustworthy Human-AI interaction, natural-language data access, behavioral-data architecture, visual analytics, and decision support.

His academic background in Visual Communication shapes how he approaches technical systems: not only as computational artifacts, but as interfaces through which people interpret evidence, uncertainty, and machine-generated recommendations. His current work explores how AI systems can remain useful without becoming opaque or over-authoritative—through clarification, validation, provenance, controllability, and explicit abstention when evidence is insufficient.

He is preparing for doctoral research focused on Human-Centered AI, trustworthy and controllable AI systems, visual analytics, and—where the research setting supports it—digital health, wearable/edge AI, and multimodal human data.

# 3. Research statement

## English

My research interest is centered on a practical question: **how can intelligent systems extend human capability without obscuring evidence, uncertainty, or control?**

I am particularly interested in systems where AI participates in analysis, decision support, or interaction rather than acting as an invisible replacement for human judgment. This includes natural-language interfaces to structured data, agentic workflows with validation and human oversight, adaptive systems that distinguish observed behavior from inferred user state, and visual interfaces that make evidence and uncertainty easier to inspect.

My engineering background leads me to study these questions as end-to-end system problems. Model behavior matters, but so do data quality, provenance, permissions, interaction design, failure handling, latency, observability, and the moment at which a system should clarify, defer, or abstain.

The research directions I want to develop further are:

1. Human-Centered AI and Human-AI Interaction
2. Trustworthy, controllable, and agentic AI systems
3. Visual analytics and AI-assisted decision support
4. Behavioral-data systems and explainable personalization
5. Privacy-aware local and edge AI
6. Digital health and wearable AI as a developing research direction

برای من، هوش مصنوعی زمانی ارزشمندتر است که در تحلیل، پشتیبانی از تصمیم یا تعامل با انسان مشارکت کند، نه این‌که به‌صورت نامرئی جای قضاوت انسانی را بگیرد. این نگاه شامل رابط‌های زبان طبیعی برای داده‌های ساختاریافته، جریان‌های عامل‌محور همراه با اعتبارسنجی و نظارت انسانی، سامانه‌های تطبیقی که رفتار مشاهده‌شده را از برداشت سیستم درباره کاربر جدا نگه می‌دارند، و رابط‌های بصری برای فهم بهتر شواهد و عدم‌قطعیت است.

پیش‌زمینه مهندسی من باعث می‌شود این مسائل را در سطح یک سامانه کامل بررسی کنم. رفتار مدل مهم است، اما کیفیت داده، منشأ و ردپای اطلاعات، مجوزها، طراحی تعامل، مدیریت خطا، تأخیر، مشاهده‌پذیری و این‌که سیستم در چه زمانی باید سؤال بپرسد، تصمیم را به انسان واگذار کند یا از پاسخ خودداری کند نیز به همان اندازه اهمیت دارند.
